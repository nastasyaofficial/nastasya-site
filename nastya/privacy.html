Privacy

Your privacy

This page explains the optional audience measurement used on this website. Analytics is off by default and is loaded only after you select “Allow analytics” in the notice.

What analytics measures

If you opt in, Google Analytics 4 may receive information such as pages viewed, approximate device and browser information, scrolling, outgoing-link clicks, and the location of a Telegram button you clicked. Campaign labels in the page URL (for example, links used in different social profiles) may also be included. The site does not ask Google Analytics to collect your name, email, Telegram identity, or message contents.

Who processes the data

Analytics data is sent to Google Analytics, a service provided by Google. Google's handling of data is described in its Privacy Policy and information about data from sites and apps. This website does not use Google Analytics for advertising or ad personalization.

Your choice

You can choose “Only necessary” in the notice to keep analytics disabled. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the site footer. Your selection is stored in this browser. Clearing this site's local storage also clears the saved choice.

Manage analytics choice

Return to the site to review or change your choice. If analytics was previously allowed, the setting will be reset first.
